# DEEPASTRO INTELLIGENCE UPGRADE 3.0: PREDICTION & CALIBRATION POLICY

## 1. Non-Fatalistic Prediction Axiom
DeepAstro rejects deterministic fatalism. Astrological timing indicates **supportive windows, internal tendencies, and environmental pressures**, not immutable preordained fate.

## 2. Prediction Anatomy Requirements
Every forward-looking prediction generated by DeepAstro must include:
1. **Direction**: Supportive, Challenging, or Mixed.
2. **Time Window**: Explicit bounded temporal range (e.g. "Q3 2026 - Q1 2027") rather than false exactitude.
3. **Primary Factors**: Root calculation drivers (e.g. Mahadasha lord, Antardasha lord, transiting Jupiter aspect).
4. **Contradictions & Counterweights**: Opposing planetary influences that mitigate or delay the indication.
5. **Confidence Rating**: Categorical designation (`LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH`).
6. **Mandatory Disclaimer**: Clear reminder that human choice, action, and worldly circumstances determine real outcomes.

## 3. Outcome Learning & Calibration Tracking
- The system tracks user-reported outcomes (`SUCCESS`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_OCCURRED`, `UNCLEAR`).
- Brier scores and calibration curves are computed **only when statistical sample size is sufficient ($N \ge 5$)**.
- When fewer than 5 verified outcomes exist, the system states: *"Calibration metrics require a minimum of 5 verified outcomes to establish statistical significance."*
- Overfitting to single users or assuming historical patterns guarantee future occurrences is strictly forbidden. All pattern recognitions are explicitly labeled `PATTERN_OBSERVED`, never `DESTINY_PROVEN`.
