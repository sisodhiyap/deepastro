## 1. The Immutable Prediction Ledger

Every forward-looking prediction generated by DeepAstro is stamped into an immutable append-only ledger:
- `prediction_id`: Cryptographically secure unique identifier
- `user_id`: Tenant owner
- `question`: Verbatim inquiry
- `timestamp`: Creation time
- `chart_snapshot`: Authoritative snapshot fingerprint
- `dasha_snapshot`: Mahadasha, Antardasha, and Pratyantardasha active
- `transit_snapshot`: Major planetary transits active
- `systems_used`: e.g. `['PARASHARI', 'D10', 'DASHA', 'GOCHAR']`
- `rules_used`: Classical texts and astrological rules applied
- `evidence_ids`: Traceable evidence links
- `prediction_window`: Multi-scale timing window (`startDate`, `endDate`, `windowScale`, `astrologicalBasis`)
- `prediction_statement`: Grounded non-fatalistic prediction text
- `uncertainty`: Explicit limitations and planetary frictions
- `confidence_class`: `VERIFIED` | `HIGH` | `MODERATE` | `LOW` | `INSUFFICIENT`
- `created_at`: Immutable timestamp

### Strict Immutability Proof
Calling `PredictionLedger.attemptRewritePrediction(predictionId, statement)` throws `IMMUTABLE_PREDICTION_VIOLATION`. Historical prediction statements cannot be rewritten, sanitized, or retconned under any circumstance.

## 2. Seven Validated Outcome Categories

Outcomes are recorded exclusively by user feedback (`POST /api/predictions/:id/outcome`):
1. `HAPPENED`: The indicated event or psychological theme manifested within the window.
2. `PARTIALLY_HAPPENED`: The theme materialized with altered scale or scope.
3. `DID_NOT_HAPPEN`: The indicated development did not materialize.
4. `TIMING_WRONG`: The theme materialized, but outside the specified astrological window.
5. `TOO_VAGUE`: Prediction lacked actionable specificity.
6. `NOT_ENOUGH_INFORMATION`: Native cannot ascertain the outcome (e.g. external third-party events).
7. `UNKNOWN`: Inconclusive or unreported outcome.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **AI is strictly forbidden from automatically determining or modifying prediction outcomes.** Only the native's explicit report constitutes outcome truth.

## 3. Specificity Evaluation Engine

DeepAstro actively prevents Barnum-effect vagueness:
- **Vague Pattern Detection**: Automatically flags phrases such as *"You may experience changes"*, *"Things will happen"*, *"Energy is shifting"*.
- **Scored Dimensions**:
  - `domainPresent`: Career, finance, marriage, relocation, health.
  - `timeWindowPresent`: Specific months, quarters, dates, or dasha sub-periods.
  - `directionPresent`: Growth, friction, delay, consolidation, expansion.
  - `evidenceAnchorPresent`: Specific houses, dashas, transits, or karakas.
  - `strengthPresent`: Moderate, intensive, subtle, supported.
- **Enforcement**: Predictions lacking planetary anchoring receive an overall specificity score $< 0.50$ and are flagged with improvement recommendations.

## 4. Multi-Scale Prediction Windows

Supported window scales:
- `DAY`: Muhurta / lunar transit windows.
- `WEEK`: Fast planetary transits (Sun, Mars, Venus, Mercury).
- `MONTH`: Solar month / transit sign passages.
- `QUARTER`: Seasonal dasha sub-periods (Antardasha / Pratyantardasha).
- `YEAR`: Mahadasha or slow transits (Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu).
- `TIMING_INSUFFICIENT`: Returned whenever planetary data cannot reliably narrow down the inquiry window (preventing false precision).
