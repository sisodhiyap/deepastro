# DEEPASTRO 3.1 — LONGITUDINAL REASONING SPECIFICATION

## 1. Multi-Epoch Timeline Synthesis
`LongitudinalReasoningEngine` compares three temporal strata for any requested life domain:
1. **Past Milestones**: User-confirmed historical events correlated with past Dasha cycles, Varga activations, and transit triggers.
2. **Current Conditions**: Present Mahadasha, Antardasha, transiting planetary axes, and dominant divisional charts.
3. **Future Windows**: Broad seasonal windows (e.g. Q3–Q4, Next H1) characterized by planetary drivers and support classifications (`HIGH_SUPPORT`, `MIXED_SUPPORT`, `CAUTION_WINDOW`, `NEUTRAL`).

## 2. Invariant: `PATTERN_OBSERVED` vs `DESTINY_PROVEN`
- The system surfaces **observed cyclical similarities** and recurring thematic tendencies.
- The system explicitly avoids deterministic fatalism. All longitudinal outputs carry the status `PATTERN_OBSERVED`.

## 3. Historical Period Matcher (`HistoricalPeriodMatcher`)
Allows the native to test intuitive historical connections (e.g., "This feels like 2018 again"):
- Retrieves confirmed 2018 chart and transit conditions.
- Isolates:
  - **Similarities**: Shared developmental friction, house activations, or planetary returns.
  - **Decisive Differences**: Greater personal sovereignty, matured boundary clarity, and changed Dasha dignity.
  - **New Protective Factors**: Stabilizing trine transits, established professional networks, and active confirmed goals.
- Result: Demonstrates that the current cycle is an elevated harmonic rather than a repetition of past vulnerability.
