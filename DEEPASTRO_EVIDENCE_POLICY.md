# DEEPASTRO INTELLIGENCE UPGRADE 3.0: EVIDENCE & SOURCING POLICY

## 1. The Epistemic Evidence Hierarchy
To ensure absolute intellectual honesty and prevent AI speculation from being treated as calculation truth, DeepAstro enforces an 8-level epistemic hierarchy:

```
LEVEL 1: Calculated Astronomical Fact (VSOP87, ELP-2000, Ascendant, Shadbala)
LEVEL 2: Independently Verified Fact (Cross-validated against reference ephemerides)
LEVEL 3: Versioned Classical Rule (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Jaimini Sutras)
LEVEL 4: Trusted Commentary Source (Classical commentaries and lineage texts)
LEVEL 5: User-Confirmed Context (Explicitly verified real-world milestones)
LEVEL 6: Approved World Fact (Consented external geographic/relocation databases)
LEVEL 7: AI Synthesis & Interpretation (Contextual reasoning & linguistic expression)
LEVEL 8: Speculation (Prohibited from presentation as astrological truth)
```

## 2. Invariant Rules of Elevation
- **No Self-Promotion**: AI interpretation (Level 7) can never elevate itself to a calculated fact (Level 1) or a classical rule (Level 3).
- **Attribution Obligation**: Every astrological assertion must cite its calculation source or classical rule ID.
- **Source Comparison**: When classical authorities conflict (e.g., conflicting house placement results in BPHS vs Phaladeepika), both positions must be surfaced with the selected methodology explicitly justified.
