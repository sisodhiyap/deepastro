## 1. Executive Summary

Astrological knowledge bases are prone to dogmatic, absolutist, or fraudulent claims injected by bad actors or hallucinating AI models (e.g. "Saturn always causes ruin", "Every Manglik chart causes divorce").
Phase 6 implements automated knowledge poisoning defenses in `JyotishReasoningEngine.ts` and `JyotishKnowledgeGraph.ts`.

## 3. Defense Mechanisms Enforced

1. **Absolutist Token Filter:** Detection and rejection of words like `"always"`, `"guarantees"`, `"every"`, `"never"`, `"inevitable"`, and `"destroys"`.
2. **Classical Exception Rule Mandate:** Every rule submitted for verification must include an `exceptions` clause citing recognized cancellation conditions.
3. **Admin Approval Quarantine:** No rule ingested via external channels or generated by AI assistance can move from `DRAFT` to `VERIFIED` without cryptographic admin signature.
4. **Source Integrity Assertion:** Ingested claims must match a registered treatise in `JyotishSourceRegistry`.
