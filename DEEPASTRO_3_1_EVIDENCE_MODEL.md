# DEEPASTRO 3.1 — EVIDENCE & SOURCING MODEL

## 1. Multi-System Evidence Budget & Quality Tiers
Evidence signals are rated along an explicit quality hierarchy:
- `PRIMARY`: Direct house lord placement, active Mahadasha/Antardasha lord dignity, or verified natal yoga.
- `SECONDARY`: Secondary transit aspect (e.g. Jupiter 5th/9th drishti), Nakshatra sub-lord, or divisional varga confirmation (D9, D10).
- `SUPPORTING`: Chara Karaka status, Ashtakavarga bindu totals, or general Muhurta favorability.
- `CONTEXTUAL`: Real-world market facts or confirmed user milestone trends.
- `SPECULATIVE`: Unverified projections — strictly prohibited from driving conclusions.

## 2. Multi-System Convergence 2.0
Instead of naively counting systems, `EvidenceFusionEngine` weighs:
- **System Independence**: Recognizing that Parashari, Jaimini, and KP utilize distinct mathematical frameworks.
- **Factor Quality**: Prioritizing fundamental dignity over minor planetary sub-aspects.
- **Classifications**:
  - `STRONG_CONVERGENCE`: 3+ independent systems point in identical direction.
  - `MODERATE_CONVERGENCE`: 2 major systems align without active opposition.
  - `MIXED`: Balanced supportive and resistive indicators.
  - `CONTRADICTORY`: Explicit tension (e.g., career expansion indicated by Dasha, but acute delays signaled by Gochara/KP).
  - `INSUFFICIENT`: Fewer than 2 qualifying signals.
